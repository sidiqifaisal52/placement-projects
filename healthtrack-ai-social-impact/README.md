# HealthTrack AI – Social Impact Case Analysis

## Project Overview

HealthTrack AI was a university group project exploring a proposed artificial intelligence system for predictive healthcare. The project examined how AI could use health information to identify possible health risks and support earlier medical intervention.

## My Contribution

My assigned area was the social impact of the proposed system. I researched and analysed how HealthTrack AI could affect patients, healthcare professionals and wider society.

My work considered:

* Patient privacy and the protection of sensitive health data
* Fairness and possible bias in AI-generated predictions
* Accessibility for people with different needs and backgrounds
* Public trust in artificial intelligence
* The importance of human oversight in healthcare decisions
* The potential benefits and risks of predictive healthcare technology
